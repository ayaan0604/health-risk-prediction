What is this Project?

Many chronic diseases are strongly influenced by lifestyle choices.

This project demonstrates how machine learning models can estimate disease risk using demographic and lifestyle information to encourage preventive healthcare.

How It Works?

Our system analyzes key lifestyle and demographic information to estimate the risk of multiple chronic diseases using trained machine learning models. After you provide your details, the backend validates the inputs and automatically selects the relevant prediction models based on the available data. Each model processes the information independently and returns a risk score, which is then presented through an intuitive dashboard to help users better understand their potential health risks.
